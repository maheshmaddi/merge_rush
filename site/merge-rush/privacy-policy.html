Merge Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Merge Rush ("the game", "we") is a single-player puzzle game published by Mahesh Maddi. This policy explains what data the game handles.

Data the game itself stores

The game saves your progress on your own device only: the current board, scores, best score, settings (sound, music, vibration), your daily streak, missions, level and achievements, and a count of games played and ads shown (used to space ads out). This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any account. We have no servers and do not collect analytics. If you choose to use the leaderboards (below), your score is sent to Google as described there.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)

The Android app can show global leaderboards through Google Play Games Services. When you open the game, it may sign you in to Play Games automatically with the Google account on your device. After a game ends, your score is submitted to Google's leaderboards, where it is shown with your Play Games profile name and avatar to other players. Google collects and processes this data (including your Play Games player ID) under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive this data and do not run any server of our own.

  • You can decline or sign out of Play Games in the Google Play Games app or Android Settings; the game works fully without it.
  • You can manage or delete your Play Games data through your Google account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to continue a game, and
  • an occasional full-screen ad between games.

To serve and measure ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this data to show personalised ads where you have agreed to that, and for fraud prevention and measurement.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch the game shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with the shield ("Privacy settings") button at the top of the game screen.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • No ads you must watch to keep playing: the rewarded ad is always optional; declining it simply ends the game with your score.

Children

Merge Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

**Full description** (1162/4000):

Merge Rush is a fast, glowing take on the classic number-merging puzzle.

Swipe to slide every tile. Matching numbers merge and double: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8, all the way to 2048 and beyond.

★ CHAIN COMBOS
Merge on three moves in a row to trigger RUSH MODE: double points while the board burns gold. Clear several pairs in one swipe for a chain bonus.

★ FOUR POWER-UPS
• Bomb: blast any tile out of the way
• Upgrade: double a tile of your choice
• Shuffle: scatter the whole board
• Sweep: clear every one of the smallest tiles
Charge them up by merging; the energy meter fills faster during Rush.

★ LAST-SECOND RESCUES
Stuck with no moves? Fire a power to break free. You get three rescues per game, and any charges you save are cashed in for bonus points at the end.

★ BEAUTIFUL AND SMOOTH
Neon gem tiles, particle bursts, satisfying sounds and haptics, tuned to run smoothly even on older phones.

★ PLAY ANYWHERE
No account, no sign-in. Your game saves automatically and plays offline.

Merge Rush is free to play with optional ads: you can watch a short ad to continue a game once, and an occasional ad appears between games. Ads never interrupt a move.

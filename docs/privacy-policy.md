# Merge Rush — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Merge Rush ("the game", "we") is a single-player puzzle game published by
Mahesh Maddi. This policy explains what data the game handles.

## Data the game itself stores

The game saves your progress on your own device only: the current board,
scores, best score, settings (sound on/off) and a count of games played and
ads shown (used to space ads out). This data never leaves your device, is not
sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any account. We
have no servers and do not collect analytics.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

- an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to continue a game, and
- an occasional full-screen ad between games.

To serve and measure ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect and process data
such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate
approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with
ads. Google may use this data to show personalised ads where you have agreed
to that, and for fraud prevention and measurement.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### Your choices

- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland):** on first launch the game shows Google's
  consent form. You can change your choice at any time with the shield
  ("Privacy settings") button at the top of the game screen.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of
  personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by
  device).
- **No ads you must watch to keep playing:** the rewarded ad is always
  optional; declining it simply ends the game with your score.

## Children

Merge Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from them.
